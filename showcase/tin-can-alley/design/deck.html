Step right up · An IWSDK showcase booth

Tin ✶ Can ✶ Alley

A neon-lit carnival booth in VR: grab softballs — by hand or force-pull — and hurl them at a pyramid of tin cans. Knock them tumbling to score, clear the shelf before your balls run out, slap RESET, go again.

Immersive VR · Quest-class Browser-testable · mouse ray ~3-minute toy · ~30 s rounds
02 · Fantasy & Pillars

You are the arm the carnival fears

⚡

Ten seconds to fun

No menus, no tutorial. A ball is in reach the moment the scene loads and the cans are begging for it.

So: first throw possible before the player has read anything — the booth teaches itself.
🥫

Physics is the toy

Every point comes from real simulated tumbling — never scripted animations. A ball clipping a can edge still feels fair.

So: no canned knockdowns; scoring reads the sim (off the shelf, or tipped past 45°).
🎪

The booth is the UI

Score lives on a panel over the booth; reset is a button you press. Nothing floats in your face.

So: zero HUD — all state readable at a glance from where you stand and throw.

The fantasy: that one perfect throw at the county fair — wind-up, release, and six cans cartwheeling off the shelf.

03 · Core Loop

Grab → Throw → Tumble → Reset

One round is 3 balls against a 6-can pyramid — roughly 30 seconds of chase-the-clear.

05 · The Space

One booth, 2.5 metres of throwing lane

Standing, stationary — no locomotion. Simplified top-down sketch; the full annotated layout lives in concept/layout.svg.

Heights

Counter 0.9 m · shelf 1.1 m · panel ≈1.9 m
Standing eye height ≈1.6 m — cans sit just below the sightline, panel just above.

Zones

Balls within arm's reach on the counter edge (M1); anything dropped is a force-pull away (M2).

Floor

Static physics plane — dropped balls rest, stray cans settle. No locomotion → no collision-geometry prerequisite.

06 · Key Moment

The last-ball clear

Your last ball catches the pyramid low and square — cans cartwheel off the shelf in a shower of clangs, the panel flips to CLEARED!, and your hand is already reaching for the reset button. Earned, not scripted: the physics did it.

10 · Tech Snapshot

One booth, whole SDK tour

Physics, grabbing, spatial UI, audio, and environment theming — exercised in a single scene. Refined in Phase 3.

Session & runtime

  • Dual runtime: ImmersiveVR primary — offer: 'once'
  • Browser-testable via canvasPointerEvents + mouse ray
  • Quest-class headset target; desktop browser for dev / CI

Features enabled

  • Physics — rigid bodies, static floor plane
  • Grabbing — one-hand near grab + distance force-pull
  • Spatial UI — PanelUI score panel, interactable reset
  • Audio + dome / IBL environment theming
  • Locomotion — none (stationary booth, comfort-safe)

Custom systems expected

  • ThrowSystem — release-velocity handoff on grip release
  • ScoringSystem — shelf-zone exit / tipped-past-45° rule
  • RoundSystem — 3-ball budget, clear/empty detection, reset

Assets & risk

  • Geometry: 100% code-built primitives — zero sourcing risk
  • Audio: guaranteed-present starter chime.mp3 (hit, clear)
  • Stretch-only dependency: a sourced whoosh SFX
